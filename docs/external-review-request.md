# External review request: MNCS 0.3-rc.1 and MNCDS 0.1-rc.1

Reviewers are asked to reproduce the release-candidate corpus offline, examine RFC
0004/0005 and the complete RC specifications, and report:

1. specification ambiguities or untestable normative language;
2. Python/Rust disagreements or unsupported rules;
3. contract/composition/freshness/lifecycle or migration defects;
4. security/privacy issues, especially omission and false-independence risks;
5. compatibility impact on historical 0.1, 0.1.1, 0.2, and draft claims; and
6. conflicts, affiliations, tool ownership, and any required recusal.

Requested evidence includes executable identity, operator identity, organization,
exact commit, environment, commands, complete output, failures, disagreements, custody
method, and whether final inputs influenced development.

Review does not authorize a release. Approvals must follow `GOVERNANCE.md`;
organizational independence and protected custody must be supported rather than
asserted.
