# Research background

The preliminary GraphFlow study tested one bounded HTTP chunked-decoder task in five
pairs. All ten final candidates passed primary and holdout correctness, and both
machine-native conditions beat the readable reference on average. The ordinary
control was faster on average than the compact agent-visible Joern condition.

Five pairs are exploratory, not statistically significant, and do not establish
generality or causation. Persistent agent-visible Joern feedback was not shown to
improve optimization. Tool-neutral analysis may still have value as an external
rejection or exception-driven repair gate.

See the [full cautious note](https://github.com/epi13/machine-native-complexity-standard/blob/main/research/graphflow-machine-native-study.md).
