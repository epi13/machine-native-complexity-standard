# MNCS research case studies

These studies exercise different machine-native complexity boundaries. A favorable development
result is not automatically a formal MNCS or MNCDS claim; each study states its own evidence and
promotion boundary.

- [EdgeStream](edgestream/README.md) — fault-tolerant C11 telemetry processing with paired
  correctness, recovery, structural, safety, and performance evidence.
- [Remote Water Control](remote-water-control/README.md) — development-only supervisory-control
  digital twin with generated planning and readable safety authority.
- [CacheForge](cacheforge/README.md) — trace-driven LLM KV-cache planning with generated eviction
  policy and readable allocation authority.
- [RAVEL](ravel/README.md) — recursive adaptive vector execution, training, temporal memory,
  planning, lifecycle, and bounded conditional compute.
- [dSense Desk Pet](dsense-desk-pet/README.md) — physical Arduino Uno study of recursive embedded
  cognition, evidence-guided acoustic repair, icon/binary representation, and severe flash limits.
