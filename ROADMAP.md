# Roadmap

MNCS 0.1 establishes testable vocabulary and bundle mechanics. Proposed next work:

- collect independent bundles across languages and risk classes;
- test provider interoperability and UNKNOWN behavior;
- add energy and reliability measurement guidance;
- evaluate deterministic regeneration and transparency-log integration;
- conduct validator security review and schema compatibility exercises;
- standardize evidence redaction without losing audit value; and
- decide through RFC evidence whether any experimental field belongs in MNCS 0.2.

No item is a promise or preapproval. Complexity scoring, mandatory proprietary
tools, and claims of universal optimality are deliberately out of scope.
