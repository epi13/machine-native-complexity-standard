# Roadmap

Validator/schema 0.1.1 completed evidence-derived conformance, certification
commands, content-addressed identities, performance derivation, cumulative level
schemas, the conformance corpus, packaged schemas, and clean-wheel verification.
The normative family remains experimental MNCS 0.1.

MNCS 0.1 establishes testable vocabulary and bundle mechanics. Proposed next work:

- signed evidence and in-toto/Sigstore-compatible attestations;
- an independent validator implementation for interoperability testing;
- multi-language provider SDKs;
- reproducible-build profiles;
- energy and hardware-utilization measurement profiles;
- external pilot components beyond the HTTP decoder task; and
- evidence redaction without losing audit value.

No item is a promise or preapproval. Complexity scoring, mandatory proprietary
tools, and claims of universal optimality are deliberately out of scope.
